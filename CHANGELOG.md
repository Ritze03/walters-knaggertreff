# Changelog

All notable user-facing changes, newest first. Categories:

**Added** (new features),
**Fixed** (bug and behaviour fixes),
**Removed** (things taken out),
**Info** (notes worth knowing).

## 2026-10-22

### Fixed
- **Minigame movement**: characters in all minigames now react instantly to your input,
  also with a slow connection.
- **Lost connection**: short drops while playing online no longer lose your moves.
- **Only Up**: easier controls with left and right only.
- **Pfandautomat**: clearer instructions and first-time hints.
- **Doodle Knagger**: smoother, and the course no longer vanishes while you watch.
- **Board**: the game board runs smoother.

## 2026-10-21

### Added
- **Second board**: a new board set at a big train station.
- **13 new minigames**: from a pigeon flying through Berlin to a rhythm game, a seat
  battle on the train and an endless subway runner.
- **Test mode**: try any minigame directly from the lobby, with bots filling the seats.
- **Bottles**: the currency is now shown as bottles everywhere.
- **Voice lines**: new German voice lines for all minigames and events.

### Fixed
- **Sound**: more balanced volume, and music that no longer drowns out the effects.
- **Text**: emoji and umlauts are no longer cut off.
- **Layout**: the top bar fits 5 and 6 players on one row, and the board labels no
  longer overlap.
- **Minigames**: Escape no longer leaves a minigame by accident.
- **Reconnecting**: players who rejoin a resumed game get a short grace period.

## 2026-10-20

### Added
- **First release**: a goofy party board game full of German memes, running in the
  browser for 2 to 6 players, each on their own PC, with keyboard or gamepad.
- **Online play**: the host creates a game and everyone else joins with a link.
- **Lobby**: players mark themselves ready, and the game starts when everyone is.
- **Minigames and board**: two minigames, a first board and voice lines to start with.
- **Hats**: a hotdog and a cucumber hat.
- **Volume slider**: adjust the volume in the game.
- **Game controller hints**: button symbols in all hints.

### Fixed
- **Path choice**: choosing a path at a fork is easier.
- **Sharper picture**: the game looks crisp on large and high-resolution screens.
- **Balance**: fairer coin and star rewards, at most one duel per round.
